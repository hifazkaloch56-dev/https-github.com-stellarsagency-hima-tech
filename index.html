How we protect your protected health information (PHI).

HIPAA Notice

Last updated: August 5, 2026

HIMA TECH RCM respects the privacy of Protected Health Information (PHI) and complies with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA) and its implementing regulations, as applicable to our role as a business associate of healthcare providers.

Our Commitment

We are committed to protecting PHI that we receive, create, maintain or transmit on behalf of our clients. We will not use or disclose PHI except as permitted by HIPAA, our business associate agreements with clients, or as required by law.

How We Use and Disclose PHI

We use PHI only to perform services on behalf of our clients, including claims submission, payment posting, coding, credentialing, eligibility verification and denial management. We may disclose PHI to subcontractors who agree to the same restrictions, and we will disclose PHI where required by law, such as for government investigations or court orders.

Security Safeguards

We implement administrative, physical and technical safeguards to ensure the confidentiality, integrity and availability of PHI, including encrypted transmissions, role-based access controls, secure storage, audit logging and ongoing workforce training on HIPAA requirements.

Breach Notification

In the event of an impermissible use or disclosure of unsecured PHI, we will notify the affected client without unreasonable delay, consistent with HIPAA's breach notification requirements, so that our clients can meet their own notification obligations.

Your HIPAA Rights

If you are a patient of one of our clients, your HIPAA rights, including the rights to access, amend, and receive an accounting of disclosures of your health information, are administered by your healthcare provider. Please contact your provider directly to exercise those rights.

Complaints

You may file a complaint with us or with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights if you believe your privacy rights have been violated. We will not retaliate against anyone who files a complaint in good faith.
