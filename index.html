About Hima Tech Health Solutions

Meet the team that treats your revenue cycle like patient care.

Our Story

Hima Tech Health Solutions was founded by healthcare revenue cycle veterans who saw the same problem everywhere: brilliant clinicians losing hard-earned revenue to messy billing. We started as a small remote-first team in 2020, taking on the toughest claims for a handful of specialty practices.

Today we partner with 500+ providers across the country — multi-specialty groups, hospitals, and solo practices alike. We have never outsourced our work offshore, and we never will. Every claim is worked by a dedicated, US-based specialist who knows your payers, your codes and your patients.

Our philosophy is simple: transparency, accuracy and relentless follow-up. If we wouldn't trust the process with our own practice, we won't ask you to.

Our Mission

"To simplify healthcare revenue management so providers can focus entirely on delivering exceptional patient care — with zero compromise on accuracy, compliance or speed."

Our Vision

"A healthcare system where every rendered service is paid correctly, the first time — and no provider loses a single dollar to preventable denials."
