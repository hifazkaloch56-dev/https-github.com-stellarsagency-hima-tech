Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about medical billing with us.

Your account number is printed on the top-right corner of your paper statement and in every email reminder we send. If you still cannot find it, call us at 1-800-MEDIBILL and we'll help right away.

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal. All payments run through a PCI-DSS compliant gateway, and we never store your full card number on our servers.

Yes. Your data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We are HIPAA compliant for protected health information and PCI-DSS certified for payment data. Full card numbers are never visible to our staff.

Contact our billing department within 30 days of the statement date. We will review the charges and, if verified, adjust the balance. You can reach us at billing@medibillpro.com or 1-800-MEDIBILL.
